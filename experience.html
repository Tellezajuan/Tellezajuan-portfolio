Selected work

Three things from the day job I’m proud of.

Company details stay private, so some results are shown as a change rather than a raw figure.

HMH · 2022 – now
Full-funnel demand generation tied to pipeline and revenue goals The starting point. Several brands ran paid media, email, and owned channels across markets, and each team and agency reported its own way, so it was hard to tell which programs moved pipeline. What I built. Paid media plans from goals and budget to creative, nurture programs mapped to the customer journey, and one monthly report in GA4, Salesforce, Marketo, and Power BI that Product Marketing, Field Marketing, Sales, Marketing Operations, and our agencies all worked from. Result. MQL-to-SQL conversion rose from 14% to 22%, spend shifted toward the programs that drove pipeline, and the work led to two promotions.
PalmPons · 2021 – 2022
Running $65K+ a month across client accounts with a team of four The starting point. Each of our 12 client accounts had its own naming, bidding, and reporting setup, so results were hard to compare and quality depended on who owned the account. What I built. Shared campaign standards that each of the four marketers worked to on their own accounts, and performance audits that turned into recommendations clients could act on. Result. 11 of 12 clients stayed through the year, and the portfolio’s average cost per lead fell 23%.
EZLeads · 2020 – 2021
Paid social for car dealerships The starting point. Most dealership campaigns ran one ad to everyone, and few leads became showroom visits. What I built. Targeting rebuilt around audience segments, and new creative tested on a regular schedule instead of a single version. Result. A seven-figure pipeline in my first year. Cost per lead fell from $38 to $21, and 18% of leads booked an appointment.
