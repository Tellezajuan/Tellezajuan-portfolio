Before the résumé

Demand generation, in plain terms.

It's the part of marketing that decides where advertising money goes and whether it's actually working. I plan the campaigns, watch the numbers as they come in, and support lead scoring with the context Sales needs. Sales owns final qualification and handoff. The hard part isn't running an ad. It's trusting the report that says the ad worked.

Why that's relevant to everything else here Almost every project on this site is a version of that same question: whether a number can be trusted, and what to do once you're not sure.

Why it's on this site

The projects extend the day job: the same questions, off the clock.

Deciding which marketing effort gets credit, checking whether a report tells a fair story, and choosing what happens next—that is a normal Tuesday at work. The marketing-measurement study on this site is the same question, run on public data instead of an internal dashboard, with the method written down instead of kept in a slide deck.

I'm not claiming the study fixed a specific problem at a specific job. I'm saying the overlap isn't a coincidence. It's the question I already ask, built out somewhere a stranger can check my work.
