Marketing Measurement

Checks whether reports counted conversions that arrived after the budget decision.

I built a plain-language way to compare what a report says today with what a marketer could have known when the budget decision was made. This is the closest match to my day job and the easiest place to start.

Automation & Operations

Case study

Job Search Workflow

Narrows job postings to a shortlist worth reading.

It reads many sources and narrows the list against a written profile. The useful output is a smaller shortlist that I can actually read and evaluate.

683 roles screened; 57 worth reading first.

I play the games and choose what is worth sharing. The workflow handles the repetitive work of finding, formatting, and remembering what the channel has already used.

Research Systems

Paper-only systems built to reject weak ideas before they become action.

Case study

Options Research

A paper-only testing process designed to make ideas fail safely.

The system runs experiments, checks them against controls, and keeps the results away from real money. The useful story is the discipline around saying ‘not enough evidence.’

15,412 backtest results; 11 of 446 cleared the gate.

See the options research →
Case study

Sports Research

A testing process that writes down how a sports idea could be rejected.

This project turns a hunch into a fair test, records the answer, and refuses to call something a win just because the first result looked interesting.

814 questions logged; none cleared the bar in the August 21 paper snapshot.

Delve
