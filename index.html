Closest fit to my day job

Late-arriving conversions made reported cost per customer look about 4× too low.

In a public dataset of 675 campaigns, the report credited conversions that arrived after the budget decision was already made. Nothing necessarily broke in tracking; the report simply filed later credit into the older decision story. I wrote the plan before analysis, built the timing check, and scrambled it 999 times. The result did not come back by chance.

The 4× figure describes 25 campaigns selected after the study for their late-arriving conversions. That group was chosen after the fact; it is not a typical result across all 675.

Read the marketing measurement case →

The timing question I wanted to know what a marketer could have known when the decision was due, not what the report looked like after the data had caught up.
